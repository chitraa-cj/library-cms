Shloka / Manthra Text

Sanskrit (Devanagari)








कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् । 
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १५ ॥

English Translation








Know that action has the Veda as its origin; the Vedas has the Immutable as its source. Hence, the all-pervading Veda is for ever based on sacrifice.

Bhashyam for this Manthra
(BhashyamForShlokaManthra)
Sanskrit Commentary








तच्चैवंविधं कर्म कुतो जातमित्याह —

कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं ब्रह्म वेदः सः उद्भवः कारणं प्रकाशको यस्य तत् कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि विजानीहि । ब्रह्म पुनः वेदाख्यम् अक्षरसमुद्भवम् अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमात्मा समुद्भवो यस्य तत् अक्षरसमुद्भवम् । ब्रह्म वेद इत्यर्थः । यस्मात् साक्षात् परमात्माख्यात् अक्षरात् पुरुषनिःश्वासवत् समुद्भूतं ब्रह्म तस्मात् सर्वार्थप्रकाशकत्वात् सर्वगतम् ; सर्वगतमपि सत् नित्यं सदा यज्ञविधिप्रधानत्वात् यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १५ ॥

English Translation








And from where does such action arise? He says:

Know that action (karma) arises from Brahman (brahmodbhavaṃ). Here, Brahman means the Veda (Vedaḥ); that which is the source, the cause, and the revealer of action is the Veda—know that action to be born of the Veda. Further, the Veda, called Brahman, arises from the Imperishable (akṣara-samudbhavam). The Imperishable (akṣaram) is the Supreme Self (paramātmā); that which has the Supreme Self as its source of origin is "born of the Imperishable." This refers to the Veda.

Because the Veda has emerged directly from the Imperishable known as the Supreme Self—like the breath of a man—it is all-pervading (sarvagatam), as it reveals all things. Although it is all-pervading, it is eternally (nityaṃ) and always established in sacrifice (yajñe pratiṣṭhitam), because its primary purpose is the injunction of sacrificial rites. || 15 ||
